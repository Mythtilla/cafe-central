01 — The house

A coffee house, not a coffee machine.

Café Central opened in 1955 and has never moved. It sits in the lower part of the old town, on Rua 21 de Agosto, between the Mercado dos Produtores and the town hall.

One room, indoors, no terrace, the counter at the back and marble worn smooth by decades of elbows. It belongs to the Rota dos Cafés com História, the trail that gathers Viseu's old cafés.

No artificial hurry, no loud music. A properly pulled bica, a pastel de nata, and room to stay five minutes — or an hour.

03 — The walk

Everything that matters is on foot.

The café sits in the lower part of the old town. From the market to the cathedral, all of it on foot — and the way passes the door.

Viseu is seen slowly. The Central is two minutes from the town hall and the Mercado dos Produtores, five from the cathedral. You need no car for any of it.
